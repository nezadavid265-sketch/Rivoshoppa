Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a delivery take?

Standard deliveries typically arrive within 24 hours. Express deliveries are completed within 2 hours. Delivery times depend on location and current demand.

What's your cancellation policy?

You can cancel orders up to 30 minutes after placing them for a full refund. After that, a 10% cancellation fee applies. Contact support for special cases.

How do I follow up on my order?

Contact our support team directly and we'll help you with updates, delays, or any questions about your delivery.

Is my package insured?

Standard insurance is included for packages up to 50,000 RWF. For higher value items, consider our premium protection plan at checkout.

Can I schedule a delivery in advance?

Yes! You can schedule deliveries up to 7 days in advance. Select your preferred date and time during checkout. Scheduled deliveries have a small additional fee.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Mobile Money (MTN, Airtel), Credit/Debit cards, and Bank Transfers. All payments are secure and encrypted.
