How it works

Every delivery, checked in at four stops.

  1. SS-01

    Request

    Tell us pickup, drop-off, and what's moving. Get a flat quote in seconds — no surge pricing.

  2. SS-02

    Pickup

    A verified driver collects the item and confirms it in the app with a photo and timestamp.

  3. SS-03

    In transit

    Watch the route live and message your driver directly if anything changes.

  4. SS-04

    Delivered

    Recipient confirms with a signature or code. You get a receipt with the full route on record.
